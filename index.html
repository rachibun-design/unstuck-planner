Your 3-Day Micro-Experiment

None of these steps require you to make a final decision. You are only collecting data.

Day 1: Low-Effort Research 10 minutes

Watch or scan one low-stakes resource

Spend exactly 10 minutes gathering concrete details. Stop when the timer dings.

10:00
Why this works: Keeping it strictly to 10 minutes stops overthinking spirals and teaches your mind that looking at facts is safe.
Day 2: Micro-Consultation 3 Pros and Cons or 1 Quick Message

Draft a 3-point list or text 1 person

Write down 3 quick pros and 3 quick cons, or send a brief 2-sentence note to someone with firsthand experience.

3 Quick Pros
3 Quick Cons
"Hi! I am gently exploring this choice and remembered your experience. If you ever have five minutes sometime next week, I would love to ask one quick question with zero pressure."
Why this works: Externalizing thoughts onto paper removes the mental loop from your head and grounds it into reality.
Day 3: Low-Stakes Action 15 minutes

Complete a 15-minute imperfect draft

Take one physical micro-action for 15 minutes. Create a deliberately messy first draft, outline, or experiment. Perfection is strictly forbidden here.

15:00
Why this works: Doing messy work directly disarms perfectionism and proves that you survive the vulnerability of making mistakes.
