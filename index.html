Your 3-Day Micro-Experiment

Remember: You are not making a final lifetime decision. You are only collecting data.

Day 1: Low-Effort Research (10 Mins)

Watch 1 casual video or read 1 short overview article about this option. Stop when your 10-minute timer dings!

Day 2: Quick Micro-Consultation

Write down 3 quick pros and 3 quick cons, or send a brief 2-sentence note to someone who has done this before.

Day 3: Low-Stakes 15-Minute Draft

Spend 15 minutes working on a rough, messy draft or initial step. Perfection is completely forbidden!

🌙 Evening Self-Trust Check-in

How did trying this experiment make you feel today?
